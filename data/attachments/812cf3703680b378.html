PUT to https://jsonplaceholder.typicode.com/posts/1

Body

{
    "userId": 1,
    "id": 1,
    "title": "Updated Post Title",
    "body": "This is the fully updated body of post 1."
}

Headers

Accept: application/json, application/javascript, text/javascript, text/json
Content-Type: application/json

Curl

curl -v -X PUT 'https://jsonplaceholder.typicode.com/posts/1' -H 'Accept: application/json, application/javascript, text/javascript, text/json' -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{ "userId": 1, "id": 1, "title": "Updated Post Title", "body": "This is the fully updated body of post 1." }'
